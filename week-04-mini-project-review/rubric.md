# Mini Project 1: What "Good" Looks Like
No formal grades, only for practice beyond your busy schedules!

| Dimension | Developing | Solid | Strong |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The question** | Given/obvious, answerable by sorting a column | Specific and non-obvious | Specific, non-obvious, group can explain why it matters |
| **Feature engineering** | No new column, or one that just relabels existing data | One meaningful engineered column | Engineered column drives the central finding |
| **Analytical thinking** | Reports a pattern, no alternative explanations considered | Names one alternative explanation | Actively tests or rules out an alternative using the data |
| **Limitations** | Not mentioned | Mentioned briefly | Specific, and the group explains how it would change their confidence |
| **Dashboard** | Visuals present but unlabeled or hard to read cold | Clear, labeled, readable without narration | Readable cold and the design choices support the claim |
| **Communication** | Claim unclear or buried | Claim stated clearly | Claim stated in the first 30 seconds, strongest evidence led with |
