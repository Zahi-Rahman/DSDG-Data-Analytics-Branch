# Mini-Project 1 Brief

**Due:** presented in-session, Week 4. **Team size:** your Week 2–3 group (3–4 people). **Dataset:** `recent-grads.csv` — an additional public dataset is fine if you want to extend the analysis, but it's optional.

## The ask
Answer a question about college major choice and outcomes that you actually find interesting. Build on something your group started in Week 2 or 3, or take a new angle.

## Requirements
- One clear claim, stated as a sentence.
- At least one engineered column — something you computed, not a raw column.
- A short Excel analysis with visible formulas, not pasted values.
- A Tableau dashboard: at least 3 linked visuals and one filter.
- One named limitation — a small sample, a possible confound, a missing variable. Naming yours is part of the deliverable, not an admission of failure.

## Avoid
- A sorted list with no engineered column and no "why does this matter."
- Presenting a correlation as if it settles a causal question.
- Copying the Week 3 demo — that was to teach the tool, not to be your answer.

## Presenting
4–5 minutes, dashboard on screen, claim in the first 30 seconds. See `rubric.md` for what officers are listening for.
