# Mini Project 1 Brief

Due: presented in session, week 4. Team size: your week 2 and 3 group, 3 to 4 people. Dataset: nps_units.csv, from the week 2 folder. You can pull in one extra public dataset if you want to extend the analysis, but it is optional.

## The ask

Answer a question about the National Park Service system that you actually find interesting. Build on something your group started in week 2 or week 3, or take a new angle entirely.

## Requirements

A clear claim, stated as a full sentence.

At least one engineered column, something you computed yourself, not a raw column read straight off the sheet.

A short Excel analysis with visible formulas, not pasted values.

A Tableau dashboard with at least three linked visuals and one filter.

One named limitation. A small number of units in a category, a possible confound, something the data cannot tell you. Naming yours is part of the deliverable, not an admission that something went wrong.

## What to avoid

A sorted list with no engineered column and no explanation of why it matters.

Presenting a pattern as if it proves a cause.

Copying the exact demo built in week 3. That was to teach the tool, not to hand you your answer.

## Presenting

Four to five minutes, dashboard on screen, your claim stated in the first thirty seconds. See rubric.md for what officers will be listening for.
